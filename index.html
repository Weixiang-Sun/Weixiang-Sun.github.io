Ph.D. student, Computer Science and Engineering
University of Notre Dame · Yes-Lab

Hi! I'm Weixiang Sun, a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame, where I am advised by Yanfang (Fanny) Ye in the Yes-Lab. I received my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from Northeastern University in 2025. At the end of my freshman year, I was selected as an exchange student at NCSU. I later conducted a research internship at Lehigh University under the supervision of Lichao Sun.

My research interests lie in Agentic AI. Currently, I am focusing on making multi-agent systems more reliable and eliciting collective intelligence that surpasses human organizations. I am also exploring multi-modal multi-agent systems.

If you're interested in my work or would like to collaborate, feel free to reach out.
